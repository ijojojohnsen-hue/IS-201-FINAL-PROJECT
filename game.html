The Baoulé Trail

Lead a migrating community westward through forest, river, and settlement routes in Côte d’Ivoire. This is a historical-fiction strategy game inspired by the Baoulé migration tradition associated with Queen Abla Pokou.

Game Rules

  • You can only camp in settlements.
  • You can only trade in settlements unless a special trade event appears.
  • Watch for rivers, elephant herds, illness, spirit trouble, and encounters with other communities.
  • Cloth helps in trade, ceremony, and diplomacy.
  • If health or morale reaches 0, the journey ends.

Settlements on the Route

Abengourou Dimbokro Yamoussoukro Bouaké

River obstacles appear around the Comoé and Bandama portions of the route.

Strategy Hints

  • Do not push forward every turn.
  • Save medicine for bad luck.
  • Use cloth wisely during ceremony or peace events.
  • Scout before dangerous territory.
  • When you reach a settlement, recover there.
